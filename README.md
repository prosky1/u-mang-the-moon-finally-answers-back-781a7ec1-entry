Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1): one line of text, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon finally answering the wishes, howls, and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I control your oceans, your months, and your werewolves. You control the remote. Fair trade.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
